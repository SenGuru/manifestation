# Privacy Policy for Mistel

**Last Updated:** [Date]

## Introduction

Welcome to Mistel ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information You Provide
We collect information that you voluntarily provide when using Mistel:

- **Account Information:** Email address, username, password (encrypted)
- **Profile Information:** Display name, optional profile photo
- **User-Generated Content:**
  - Daily affirmations you create
  - Vision boards (images and text)
  - Goals and goal progress
  - Journal entries
  - Meditation preferences

### Automatically Collected Information
When you use Mistel, we automatically collect:

- **Usage Data:** Features used, session duration, meditation time
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers
- **Analytics:** App performance, crash reports, feature usage statistics

### Camera and Photo Library
- **Camera Access:** Only used when you choose to take photos for vision boards
- **Photo Library Access:** Only used when you select existing photos for vision boards
- We do NOT access your photos without explicit permission for each use

### Microphone
- **Microphone Access:** Only requested if you use voice-recording features
- We do NOT record audio without your explicit consent

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

1. **Provide and Maintain the App**
   - Create and manage your account
   - Store your affirmations, vision boards, goals, and journal entries
   - Sync your data across devices
   - Provide customer support

2. **Improve Our Services**
   - Understand how users interact with Mistel
   - Fix bugs and improve performance
   - Develop new features
   - Conduct analytics and research

3. **Communication**
   - Send you app-related notifications
   - Respond to your inquiries
   - Send important updates about the app
   - Marketing communications (with your consent)

## Data Storage and Security

### Cloud Storage
- Your data is securely stored on our servers
- We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS)
- Vision boards and photos are stored securely with restricted access

### Local Storage
- Some data is cached locally on your device for offline access
- Local data is protected by your device's security features

### Security Measures
- Passwords are encrypted using industry-standard hashing
- All data transmission uses secure HTTPS protocols
- Regular security audits and updates
- Restricted access to user data

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

We **DO NOT** sell your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information only in these limited circumstances:

1. **Service Providers**
   - Cloud hosting providers (for data storage)
   - Analytics providers (for app improvement)
   - Customer support tools
   - All service providers are contractually obligated to protect your data

2. **Legal Requirements**
   - If required by law or legal process
   - To protect our rights, property, or safety
   - To prevent fraud or security issues

3. **Business Transfers**
   - In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets
   - Users will be notified of any change in data ownership

## Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

1. **Access Your Data**
   - Request a copy of all data we have about you

2. **Correct Your Data**
   - Update or correct inaccurate information

3. **Delete Your Data**
   - Request deletion of your account and all associated data
   - Delete individual vision boards, goals, or journal entries within the app

4. **Export Your Data**
   - Download your data in a portable format

5. **Opt-Out**
   - Unsubscribe from marketing emails
   - Disable analytics tracking (though this may limit app functionality)

To exercise these rights, contact us at: privacy@mistel.app

## Data Retention

- **Active Accounts:** We retain your data as long as your account is active
- **Deleted Accounts:** Data is permanently deleted within 30 days of account deletion
- **Backups:** Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 90 days
