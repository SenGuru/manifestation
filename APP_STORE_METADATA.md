## App Description (4000 characters max)

Transform your life with Mistel, the ultimate manifestation and meditation app designed to help you achieve your dreams through the power of positive thinking, visualization, and daily practice.

**KEY FEATURES:**

🌟 **Daily Affirmations**
- Personalized affirmations across 10+ categories including Love, Wealth, Health, Success, and Confidence
- Voice narration for hands-free listening
- Save your favorite affirmations for quick access
- Custom affirmation creation

🎨 **Vision Board Creator**
- Create beautiful digital vision boards with photos and text
- Drag, resize, and customize elements with intuitive controls
- Multiple boards for different life goals
- Cloud sync to access your boards across devices
- Export and share your vision boards

🎯 **Goal Tracker**
- Set and track SMART goals across all areas of your life
- Monitor progress with visual indicators
- Break down large goals into actionable steps
- Celebrate milestones and achievements

📔 **Manifestation Journal**
- Daily journaling with guided prompts
- Gratitude logging to cultivate abundance mindset
- Track synchronicities and manifestations
- Reflect on your journey

🧘 **Visual Meditation**
- Guided visual meditations with beautiful animations
- Choose from Candle, Moon, Nature, Geometric, or Minimal visuals
- Customizable duration (5-30 minutes)
- Ambient soundscapes including Nature, Binaural Beats, Singing Bowls, and more
- Perfect for beginners and experienced meditators

📊 **Usage Analytics**
- Track your meditation and journaling streaks
- View your progress over time
- Stay motivated with achievement insights
